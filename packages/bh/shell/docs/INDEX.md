## Shell

- [AppShell](../docs/app-shell.md): Put every page of an app in one frame: a sidebar of its nav, a top bar and a drawer on a phone, and its search.
- [Navigation data](../docs/navigation-data.md): Describe the app's nav as sections of destinations, with counts, dots, statuses and pages of their own.
- [Sidebar parts](../docs/sidebar-parts.md): The pieces AppShell is built from, for an app that assembles a frame of its own.
- [Theme](../docs/theme.md): Light, dark or the OS's, stamped on the page's root, remembered under the app's own key, and changed from the sidebar.
- [Pages](../docs/pages.md): Frame a page with Page and PageHeader, lay its body out in a PageBody or a CardGrid, and give it tabs that are routes.
- [SearchDialog](../docs/search-dialog.md): Search what the app holds in a dialog: type, move with the arrows, open with Enter.
- [OnThisPage](../docs/on-this-page.md): List a page's sections beside it, the one being read lit as the page scrolls, and go to one with a click.
- [Pager](../docs/pager.md): Link a page to the pages either side of it in reading order, at its foot.
- [Version](../docs/version.md): Show the stack's version under the app's name, and open the Version page from it: services, packages and every release's commits.
