## Templates

- [Overview](../docs/overview.md): A template says what a record shows, as JSON. A table cell, a list item, a tile and a card body are templates.
- [Layouts](../docs/layouts.md): A layout places a template's nodes. bh registers its own; an app adds more.
- [Renderers](../docs/renderers.md): A renderer draws a part's value. bh registers its own; an app adds more.
- [Formats](../docs/formats.md): A format writes a value as text. bh registers its own; an app adds more.
